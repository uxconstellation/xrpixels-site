Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

This policy covers xrpixels.com and the proposal pages we host at xrpixels.win. It is written to describe what actually happens on these sites, not to cover things we do not do.

The short version

We collect nothing about you unless you type it into our contact form. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking of any kind on this site.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your company name
  • Your phone number
  • Your e-mail address
  • Whatever you write in the message field

We do not set cookies, we do not store anything in your browser, and we do not run analytics or advertising scripts.

What we do with it

Contact form submissions are delivered to us by e-mail at marketing@xrpixels.com. We use them to reply to your enquiry and, if we go on to work together, to run that relationship. We do not sell your information, we do not rent it, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

Services that see your request

Loading any website means your device makes a connection, and the companies that serve it can see technical details such as your IP address and browser. For this site those are:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts and delivers the site and processes the contact form
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare, which serve the typefaces
  • unpkg, which serves the animation library
  • Vimeo, which serves an embedded video where one appears

Each of these operates under its own privacy policy. We do not pass them anything about you beyond what your browser sends to fetch the file.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our e-mail for as long as they are useful to the conversation or to a working relationship, and for as long as we need them for tax and record-keeping. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Your choices

Write to marketing@xrpixels.com and you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. We will act on it. Depending on where you live, you may have these as legal rights, including under the GDPR in the UK and Europe and the CCPA in California.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please do not send us anything confidential through the contact form. E-mail us and we will find a better route.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Client proposal pages

Some pages on xrpixels.win are protected by a password we give to a specific client. Those pages set one cookie, purely to remember that you entered the password correctly so you are not asked again on every page. It holds no personal information and it is not used for tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
